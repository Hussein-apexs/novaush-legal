Medical emergencies

NovaUsh is not an emergency service. If you think you are having a medical emergency, contact your local emergency number immediately.

Questions about your care

For questions about your treatment, medication or appointments, message your care team from inside the app.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the profile menu and choose to delete your account. Prescriptions, clinician notes and medical history are kept for the period required by healthcare law.

Notifications and permissions

You can change camera, photo library, microphone and notification permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings app under NovaUsh.
